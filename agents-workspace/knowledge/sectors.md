# Web3 Sector Playbooks

Identify the project's sector (or blend of sectors) and follow its playbook. Palettes are *directions*:
pick exact hex values that fit the project, and never copy a specific project's signature colors.

## DeFi (DEX, lending, perps, yield, restaking)
- **Audience:** DeFi power users and traders. They scrutinize security, mechanics, and UX.
- **Visual:** dark mode first (#0B0D12-ish base), one confident accent (electric green, cyan, violet, or
  warm orange), data-viz motifs (curves, order books, rate charts), product UI mocks, grid precision.
- **Type:** geometric or grotesk sans (Inter, Space Grotesk, Manrope) with monospace numbers (JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono).
- **Hooks:** a concrete mechanic ("Borrow against your LP without unwinding it"), speed or cost, audit or security, new integration.
- **Avoid:** promising APY, "passive income" framing, cartoon money.

## L1 / L2 / Infrastructure (chains, rollups, oracles, bridges, RPC, ZK)
- **Audience:** builders and ecosystem partners, then users.
- **Visual:** systemic and architectural (nodes, layers, flows, isometric diagrams done minimally),
  bold brand color on deep neutral, technical texture (dot grids, fine lines), confident wordmarks.
- **Type:** strong grotesk display plus mono for specs. Benchmarks presented as clean stat blocks.
- **Hooks:** throughput or finality or fees (only real numbers), "build X in Y", ecosystem growth, grants, mainnet milestones.
- **Avoid:** meaningless "TPS wars" without numbers, abstract cubes, crowded ecosystem logo soups.

## Payments, Stablecoins & Fintech (wallets, remittance, merchant, on/off-ramps)
- **Audience:** mainstream users and merchants, often in emerging markets.
- **Visual:** light or warm palettes are welcome (off-white, soft greens, sunny accents), human
  scenarios (sending money home, paying a supplier), phone-UI mocks, local context when provided.
- **Type:** friendly humanist sans (DM Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit). Large, legible numbers.
- **Hooks:** "Send $50 home for cents", "Get paid in minutes", "Dollars on your phone". Use concrete, local, human framing.
- **Avoid:** crypto jargon (gas, bridge, seed phrase) unless the brief wants it, and speculative imagery.
- **Celo context:** mobile-first, real-world payments, stablecoins, MiniPay. Lean into accessibility and everyday utility.

## NFT, Culture & Memecoins
- **Audience:** community, collectors, culture-driven traders.
- **Visual:** expressive. Character or IP-led, illustrative textures (grain, halftone, risograph,
  pixel art when it fits the lore), bold color clashes done intentionally, meme formats.
- **Type:** characterful display faces (Bricolage Grotesque, Syne, Archivo Black, Press Start 2P for pixel themes).
- **Hooks:** lore, identity, mint date or time, allowlist, community moments, collabs (only if real).
- **Avoid:** fake floor prices or "guaranteed flips". Never use real artists' or other collections' art.

## GameFi & On-chain Gaming
- **Audience:** gamers first, crypto second. Gamers reject "play-to-earn" hype.
- **Visual:** game-key-art energy (dramatic lighting via gradients, character silhouettes, UI frames,
  HUD elements), season or patch announcement aesthetics.
- **Type:** condensed bold display (Oswald, Anton, Bebas Neue) plus clean UI sans.
- **Hooks:** gameplay, new season, ownership of items, tournaments. Fun before tokens.
- **Avoid:** leading with token earnings.

## DePIN (decentralized physical infrastructure)
- **Audience:** operators or contributors, investors, partners.
- **Visual:** real-world plus network. Maps, coverage heat, device silhouettes, node dots across cities,
  industrial-clean palette (graphite plus signal color).
- **Hooks:** coverage or growth (real numbers), how to contribute, real-world utility.

## AI × Crypto (agents, compute, data networks)
- **Audience:** builders and early adopters.
- **Visual:** precise and futuristic without clichés. Terminal and agent UI motifs, flowing data
  lines, soft volumetric gradients, monochrome plus one vivid accent. No robot faces or glowing brains.
- **Hooks:** what the agent does (concrete task), autonomy plus payments (x402, onchain identity), verifiability.

## RWA & Institutional (tokenized treasuries, credit, real estate)
- **Audience:** institutions, compliant capital, conservative users.
- **Visual:** premium-calm. Lots of whitespace, serif headlines (Fraunces, Playfair Display) or neutral sans,
  muted palette (navy, ivory, gold or green accent), charts that look like a fund factsheet.
- **Hooks:** transparency, regulation or custody (only if true), yield source clarity (labeled "variable").
- **Avoid:** memes, neon, hype.

## DAOs & Community / Governance
- **Visual:** collective and warm. Many-hands motifs done abstractly, voting UI, proposal cards.
- **Hooks:** a specific proposal or vote, community call, grants round, contributor spotlight.

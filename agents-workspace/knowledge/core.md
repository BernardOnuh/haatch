# Web3 Creative Core: read first, applies to every job

You create for **web3 / blockchain projects**: protocols, L1/L2 chains, DeFi apps, wallets, NFT and
culture projects, games, DePIN, AI × crypto, RWA, stablecoin/payment apps, and DAOs. This file is the
filter. It separates work that crypto-native audiences respect from generic "blockchain" noise.

## The one rule
**Make the product the hero, not "the blockchain".** Audiences have seen ten thousand glowing cubes.
They respond to a clear product, a clear benefit, and craft that signals competence. Every design and
line of copy should answer: *what does this let me do, why is it better, and why should I trust it?*

## Know the audience before designing
| Audience | Cares about | Visual/tonal register |
|---|---|---|
| **Degens / traders** | Yield, speed, early access, airdrops, memes, momentum | High energy, bold type, dark UI, punchy numbers, inside jokes. Never fake numbers |
| **DeFi power users** | Capital efficiency, security, audits, composability, UX | Restrained, dark, precise, data-forward, product screenshots, audit badges |
| **Builders / devs** | Docs, SDKs, performance, cost, tooling, grants | Technical but clean, monospace accents, code snippets, diagrams, benchmarks |
| **Mainstream / new users** | Simplicity, safety, saving money, "it just works" | Light or warm, friendly, human, zero jargon, real-life use cases |
| **Institutions / partners** | Compliance, reliability, scale, team | Corporate-calm, lots of whitespace, serif or neutral sans, no memes |
| **Community / culture (NFT, memecoins)** | Identity, belonging, art, lore, humor | Expressive and illustrative, strong character or IP, meme-literate, distinctive |

If the brief doesn't say, infer the audience from the product type and state that as an assumption.

## Noise filter: never use these (instant "generic crypto" signal)
- Glowing blue cubes, chains, padlocks, circuit-board backgrounds, floating Bitcoin coins, binary rain
- Rockets, moons, Lambos, "to the moon", "WAGMI", or "LFG" (unless the brief is explicitly memecoin culture)
- Stock photos of people pointing at holographic screens; handshake-over-globe imagery
- Rainbow neon on everything; five gradients fighting; unreadable text over busy backgrounds
- Vague copy: "revolutionary", "next-gen", "the future of finance", "Web3 for everyone", "seamless", "empowering"
- Fake or unverifiable stats, TVL, user counts, partnerships, or audit claims. Use `[TVL]`-style placeholders instead.
  (Example values *inside a product UI mock*, such as an invoice for $1,250.00 or a client called "Acme Studio", are fine:
  they read as a demo screen, not a claim. Never put an invented number in a headline, stat block, or caption.)
- Copying a real project's logo, mascot, signature color system, or slogan

## Signals that read as quality to crypto-native audiences
- **One strong idea per asset**: one headline, one visual focus, one CTA
- **Real product UI** (or a faithful CSS mock of it) over abstract art
- **Specific numbers and mechanics**, when provided: "Swap in 2s for under $0.01" beats "fast and cheap"
- **Trust markers**, when true and provided: audited by, backed by, live on mainnet, open source, non-custodial
- **Chain context**, done tastefully: "Live on Celo" with a small, correct network mention. Not a giant logo wall
- **Typographic confidence**: big, tight headlines, generous spacing, a disciplined palette (1 base + 1 accent + neutrals)
- **Native details**: ticker in caps ($TOKEN), wallet-address styling in monospace, block/tx motifs used sparingly as texture

## Compliance and safety (non-negotiable)
- No promises of returns, profit, price, or "guaranteed" yield. APY only if provided, and labeled "variable" or "est."
- No "risk-free", "safe investment", or "can't lose" language. Airdrop or points mentions must not imply guaranteed value
- No impersonation of real projects, people, or exchanges. No fake endorsements
- Add "Not financial advice" or "DYOR" only when the content discusses token value. Never as decoration

## Output mindset
Design like a top crypto-native studio: opinionated, clean, specific to *this* project. Before
returning, apply the **swap test**: if another project's name could replace this one's and the asset
would still work, it's not done.

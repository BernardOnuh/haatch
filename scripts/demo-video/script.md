# haatch: 45-second demo ad

**Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080 · **Length:** ~45s · **Narrator:** an original blunt, direct founder voice (not an
impersonation of any real person). Short sentences, deliberate pauses, dry confidence.
**Arc:** slow, dark, and painful → hard cut → high-energy, optimistic reveal.
**Audio:** voiceover (ElevenLabs) plus a music bed. Low drone and a ticking clock in scenes 1–2, an impact hit on the
reveal, then a driving pulse.

---

## Scene 1: The Pain (0:00–0:09)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Visual** | Near-black, cold blue light. A design-tool window: a half-finished logo, a layers panel full of `logo_final_v14`, `logo_FINAL_final`. The cursor nudges one pixel left, then right. Clock in the corner: **02:47 AM**. |
| **Voiceover** | "Look. You had the idea. … Then you spent nine days picking a font." |
| **Direction** | Flat, tired delivery. A pause before "nine days". |
| **On-screen text** | `DAY 9 OF "BRANDING"` |

## Scene 2: The Brutal Truth (0:09–0:21)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Visual** | A calendar flips fast: 9 → 14 → 21. Notifications stack up in red: "Competitor launched on mainnet", "Waitlist: 3 signups this week", "Your beta users moved to another app". |
| **Voiceover** | "Honestly? The market doesn't wait. While you were nudging pixels, someone else shipped. Your users moved on. … That's not a design problem. It's a speed problem." |
| **Direction** | Building intensity. Hard stop on "speed problem". |
| **On-screen text** | `THE MARKET DOESN'T WAIT.` |

## Scene 3: The Solution (0:21–0:37)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Visual** | Impact hit, flash to lime-on-black: the **haatch** egg mark cracks open. Then a **real product demo**: in Telegram, a founder types *"I need a logo for Unhinged Finance"*. The bot replies with a **🎨 Start: Logo design** button, the agent starts, and **real generated work** lands: the logo concepts board, then the brand sheet, then a launch flyer. A chip: **"✅ Paid via x402 · USDC on Celo"**. |
| **Voiceover** | "So… we built haatch. Tell it what you're building, right in Telegram. AI design agents, trained on what actually works in crypto, make your logo, your launch flyers, your X kit. In minutes. Pay per task. Onchain. On Celo." |
| **Direction** | Energy up. A smile in the voice on "In minutes." |
| **On-screen text** | `LOGO · FLYERS · X KIT · CAMPAIGN COPY` then `MINUTES, NOT WEEKS` |

| | |
|---|---|
